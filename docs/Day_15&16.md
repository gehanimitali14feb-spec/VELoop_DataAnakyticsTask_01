## 2. 7-Step Python Data Cleaning & Standardization Pipeline

All raw sheets were processed through a structured 7-step pipeline in `Analyis_Cleaning.ipynb`:

1. **Step 1 (Workbook Profiling):** Programmatically audited row counts, column schemas, null distributions, and duplicate counts across all 15 sheets.
2. **Step 2 (Deduplication):** Stripped whitespace and enforced uppercase formatting on `user_id`, removing the **8 duplicate user records** in `Users_Messy_Audit` (`1,008 -> 1,000` unique users) and **10 duplicate telemetry events** in the audit log.
3. **Step 3 (Text & Category Standardization):** Normalized string casing across `gender` (`Male`, `Female`, `Prefer Not To Say`), `account_status`, `country`, `primary_device`, `platform`, `acquisition_source`, and `task_status`.
4. **Step 4 (Missing Value Imputation):**
   * Imputed `referral_source_detail` nulls with `"Direct / Organic"` and `gender` nulls with `"Unspecified"`.
   * Imputed single-record categorical nulls (`city`, `acquisition_source`, `primary_device`, `email_verification_status`, `onboarding_status`) with `"Unknown"`.
   * Imputed missing `age` with the cohort median age (**28.0 years**; range: `18` to `39` years, mean: `28.52`).
5. **Step 5 (Outlier Remediation & Chronological Validation):**
   * Clipped the **4 negative values** in `days_since_registration` to `0`.
   * Standardized `registration_datetime` and `last_login_datetime` to ISO timestamps and imputed the 73 missing login timestamps, achieving **0 remaining nulls** across the master user table.
6. **Step 6 (User-Level Feature Engineering & Rollups):** Merged per-user task funnel aggregations (`total_tasks_attempted`, `tasks_completed`, `tasks_failed`, `task_completion_rate_pct`) and daily activity retention tiers (`retention_tier`) directly into the master user dimension table (`34` total columns).
7. **Step 7 (Star-Schema Packaging):** Exported all 8 cleaned relational tables into `VELoop_PowerBI_Master_Dataset.xlsx` and `cleaned_users_audit_output.csv`.

## 4. Wallet, Reward & Telemetry Reconciliation Findings

1. **Task Reward Crediting Integrity (`05_Task_Activity`):**
   * Exactly **5,627 tasks** reached `task_status == 'Completed'`, and exactly **5,627 task records** show `reward_status == 'Credited'` (100% 1-to-1 settlement alignment between task completion and reward crediting). The remaining 4,219 incomplete/failed tasks were properly held back across pending/uncredited states.
2. **Wallet & Exchange Telemetry Blind Spot (`07_Feature_Usage` & `03_Feature_Events`):**
   * While **810 unique users** generated **2,172 `wallet_viewed` events** and **720 unique users** generated **1,690 `exchange_viewed` events**, both `Wallet` and `Exchange Center` recorded **`0` `users_with_success_event`** in the feature usage matrix because all logged events for these modules terminated at `event_status == 'Started'` (`wallet_viewed`, `exchange_viewed`) without firing downstream settlement events (`withdrawal_completed` or `exchange_completed`).
   * Across the entire telemetry log (`35,714` raw audit events), **18,842 events (52.76%)** were flagged as `reward_related_flag == 'Yes'` and **9,776 events (27.37%)** were flagged as `xp_related_flag == 'Yes'`.
   * Overall telemetry event outcomes broke down as: **`Started` (18,632 / 52.17%)**, **`Success` (14,434 / 40.42% after merging 4 lowercase `success` anomalies)**, **`Abandoned` (1,764 / 4.94%)**, and **`Failed` (884 / 2.48%)**.
